# Contribution terms

Submit material you created or have authority to contribute, including
permission from your employer or other rights holder where needed. Identify
third-party material, its upstream source, version and license in the pull
request. Preserve its notices and wait for maintainers to confirm permission
before it is incorporated. Credit prior authors and describe material changes.

For any original material you submit for inclusion, add this statement to the
pull request:

> I have the right to submit this contribution. I offer my original software,
> firmware and accompanying software documentation in this contribution to the
> maintainers and downstream recipients under the PolyForm Noncommercial License
> 1.0.0. I offer my original hardware design material in this contribution, if
> any, to the maintainers and downstream recipients under the repository's
> hardware design license. These grants include the patent permissions in the
> applicable license to the extent I have authority to grant them. I have
> identified separately licensed material and preserved its notices.

Add a `Signed-off-by: Name <email>` line to each commit using `git commit -s`.
Use an email address you are willing to make public. The sign-off records your
certification of provenance and submission authority. The pull request
statement supplies the express inbound license. Contributors retain ownership.
A future commercial license for a contribution requires separate permission from
the relevant rights holders.
